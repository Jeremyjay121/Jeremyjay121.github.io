Horror Defense Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026.

Horror Defense is developed by Iku-Games. For privacy questions, contact jeremyjay121.gamedev@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

The game stores settings, custom maps, and saved run results on your device. Android leaderboards are local to that device. You do not need to create a separate Horror Defense account to play.

When you join an online match, the host and connected players receive information needed to run the match, such as your display name, game actions, and game state. Custom maps may be transferred when connecting players accept the download. Text chat messages are transmitted to participants. Push-to-talk voice transmits audio while you use that feature and requires microphone permission. Other participants may independently retain or record information shared with them. Direct-IP multiplayer traffic is not configured with end-to-end encryption.

On Steam, optional platform features use Steam services, including multiplayer lobbies, Workshop maps, achievements, and global leaderboards. Steam handles account and platform information under its own privacy policy. Optional Discord integration may share game activity with Discord when enabled.

Advertising on Android

The free Android edition uses Google AdMob for advertising. The paid Steam edition does not show ads.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate its services. The information used depends on your consent choices, device settings, region, and Google's SDK configuration. Advertising SDK data is encrypted in transit as described by Google; this does not describe the game's direct-IP multiplayer connection.

Where required and supported by Google's consent system, the Android game requests advertising consent and provides access to privacy choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and permissions in Android settings. Test releases may display test advertisements.

Google's information about these practices is available at:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Steam's privacy policy is available at https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/ and Discord's at https://discord.com/privacy.

Support and retention

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Local game data remains on your device until removed or replaced. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local data, subject to any device backups or platform restore behavior. Third-party services retain information under their own policies. Removing local data does not delete information held by Google, Steam, Discord, or other multiplayer participants.

Your choices

You can play offline, avoid optional chat and voice features, revoke microphone permission in device settings, and use available advertising privacy controls. Contact us about privacy questions or information you have supplied directly to us. Requests involving platform account data should also be directed to the relevant platform.

Intended audience

Horror Defense is intended for players ages 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information directly to us, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The published policy will identify its effective date.
